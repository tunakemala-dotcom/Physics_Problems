# Task 05 – Percentage Calculation

## Problem Statement

A measurement of time is recorded as $t = 5.45 \pm 0.22\text{ seconds}$. What is the percentage uncertainty of this measurement?

## Theory

Percentage uncertainty is the ratio of the absolute uncertainty to the measured nominal value, expressed as a percentage.

$$
\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{\Delta t}{t} \right) \times 100\%
$$

## Step-by-Step Solution

Identify the nominal value $t = 5.45\text{ s}$ and absolute uncertainty $\Delta t = 0.22\text{ s}$.

Substitute into the formula:

$$
\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{0.22}{5.45} \right) \times 100\%
$$

$$
\text{Percentage Uncertainty} \approx 0.04036 \times 100\% \approx 4.04\%
$$

## Final Result

The percentage uncertainty of this time measurement is approximately $4.04\%$.

## Interpretation

This metric indicates the relative precision of the measurement. A $4\%$ error is typical for manual stopwatch timing due to human reaction time variability.
